# Engineering fixtures

`daily_bars.csv` is a small deterministic ingestion fixture. `research_daily_bars.csv` is synthetic and is generated by `generate_research_fixture.py`. `seed_universe.csv`, `us_sessions.csv` and `corporate_actions.csv` support local feature, calendar, accounting and risk tests. `generate_phase3_validation_artifact.py` generates validation plumbing input.

These files are not real point-in-time investment evidence, a current stock recommendation, an admissible ML training dataset or proof of >10% one-year returns. Do not use their performance to select models or approve deployment. Calendar flags are engineering fixtures, not historical calendar certification.

R3 tests may add deterministic label/split fixtures with exact expected outcomes. They must remain clearly synthetic and must not require credentials, downloads or broker connectivity. See [data requirements](../../docs/DATA_AND_FEATURES.md) and [execution plan](../../docs/ROADMAP.md).
